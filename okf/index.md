# MOX-ADV OKF

This directory is the portable living knowledge bundle for the MOX-ADV advertising-control prototype.
Its concepts are readable by humans and consumable by agents without a repository-specific runtime or SDK.
Repository-specific agent integration remains outside the bundle in `docs/agents/okf.md`.
Verify every implementation or operational decision against the cited live source before changing behavior.
The OKF bundle does not replace the normative requirements, accepted ADRs, executable schemas, tests, or current runbooks.
