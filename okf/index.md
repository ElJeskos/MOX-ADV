# MOX-ADV OKF

This directory is the portable living knowledge bundle for the MOX-ADV advertising-control prototype.
Its concepts provide progressive orientation without replacing current requirements, future accepted ADRs, executable schemas, implementation, tests, or runbooks.
Repository-specific producer and consumer workflow remains outside the bundle in `docs/agents/okf.md`.

## Navigation

- [Project](project/index.md) explains source authority, the normative baseline, and implementation preconditions.
- [Product](product/index.md) explains the proof goal, prototype boundary, and human-control model.
- [Architecture](architecture/index.md) explains the closed loop and its trust boundaries.
- [Implementation](implementation/index.md) explains required contracts, state, acceptance, and evidence.
- [Runbooks](runbooks/index.md) routes safety-sensitive external-write work.
- [References](references/index.md) maps provenance and records the v0.1 migration boundary.
